Promotions Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

These terms apply to any discount code, seasonal sale, bundle offer, loyalty credit, or other promotion offered by Freemen Provisions (each a "Promotion"). The specific details of a Promotion — including its value, eligibility, and expiry — will always be stated where the Promotion is advertised.

General conditions

  • Promotions cannot be combined unless the offer explicitly says so. One Promotion per order.
  • Promotions have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be sold or transferred.
  • Promotions apply to the product or service price only, not to shipping, handling, or taxes, unless stated otherwise.
  • Each Promotion has an expiry date. Expired Promotions cannot be applied retroactively to past orders.
  • If a Promotion is percentage-based, the discount is calculated on the pre-tax subtotal.

Returns on promotional orders

If you return part of an order that qualified for a Promotion (for example, a "buy 3, save 15%" offer), the refund is calculated on the discounted price actually paid for the returned item(s). If returning the item voids the Promotion's conditions, the refund may be reduced so the remaining items reflect their regular price.

Errors, abuse & changes

  • If a Promotion is published with a clear pricing or listing error, we may correct or withdraw it and will notify affected customers.
  • We may decline or cancel orders that abuse a Promotion (for example, creating multiple accounts to reuse a first-customer discount).
  • We may end or modify a Promotion at any time; orders already placed before the change are honored at the promoted price.

Questions

Questions about an active Promotion? Email freemenpro@pm.me or call (407) 704-0240. When no Promotion is advertised, none is active — this page still describes how any future offer will work.
