Refund & Dispute Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Refunds

We want every customer to be satisfied. Refunds are available for:

  • Returns of eligible non-perishable goods within 30 days, per our Return Policy.
  • Damaged, spoiled, or incorrect items — replaced or refunded in full, reported within 7 days of delivery.
  • Cancelled services — refunded per our Cancellation Policy.
  • Never-delivered orders — any order confirmed lost in transit is refunded in full or re-shipped, your choice.

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Card refunds typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

How to request a refund

  • Email contact@freemenprovisions.com or call (555) 013-0142 with your order number and a short description of the issue (photos help for damaged goods).
  • We acknowledge refund requests within one business day.
  • Most refunds are decided within 3 business days of the request.

Disputes (chargebacks)

If you believe a charge is incorrect and contact your card issuer to dispute it, we will:

  • Respond to the dispute through the card network with evidence of the transaction (order confirmation, delivery confirmation, and any message history).
  • Pause any collections activity while the dispute is being reviewed.
  • Refund the charge promptly if the dispute is resolved in your favor, or if we determine the charge was made in error.

Our promise: if you reach out to us first, we will always try to resolve a billing problem directly and quickly — most issues are fixed within one business day without any need for a formal dispute. Disputing a charge should be a last resort, because it can delay your refund while the card network investigates.

Billing questions

Unrecognized charge? Email us the date and amount and we will identify the transaction and send you the corresponding receipt within one business day.
