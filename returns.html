Return Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers physical products purchased from Freemen Provisions, including seasonal produce, eggs, preserves, seed and starter plants, and feed & forage blends.

30-day return window

If you're not satisfied with a non-perishable product (preserves, seed, unopened feed), you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and in resalable condition.

Perishable goods

For food-safety reasons, perishable products (fresh produce, eggs, opened feed) cannot be returned. However, if any perishable item arrives damaged, spoiled, or otherwise not right, we will replace it or refund it in full — see below.

Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

  • Report damaged, spoiled, or incorrect items within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number.
  • We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund to your original payment method — your choice.
  • No return shipment is needed for damaged or spoiled perishables.

How to start a return

  • Email freemenpro@pm.me or call (407) 704-0240 with your order number and reason for return.
  • We will reply within one business day with a return authorization and, for non-perishables, a prepaid return shipping label.
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of us receiving the returned item, or immediately for damaged/perishable claims.

Exceptions

  • Live plants that have been planted or transplanted cannot be returned, but are covered by our replacement guarantee for plants that arrive dead or unhealthy.
  • Custom-milled feed blends are made to order and cannot be returned unless defective.

For refunds and billing disagreements, see our Refund & Dispute Policy.
