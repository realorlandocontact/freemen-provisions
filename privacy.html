Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Freemen Provisions respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is protected.

What we collect

  • Order & booking information — name, email, shipping address, and phone number, used to fulfill your order or service.
  • Payment information — processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages — content you send us by email, phone, or our contact form, used to respond to you.

How we use it

  • To process and deliver orders, provide booked services, and handle returns and refunds.
  • To communicate with you about your order, booking, or inquiry.
  • To meet legal, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not sell or rent customer information to anyone, and we do not send marketing emails unless you opt in.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to run our business — payment processing (Stripe), shipping carriers, and email delivery — and only to the extent required for your order or booking. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Your choices

  • You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal information by emailing freemenpro@pm.me.
  • You may opt out of any marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

Security

Payment data is encrypted and handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Access to order records within our business is limited to those who need it to serve you.
