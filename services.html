Consulting & services

Practical help for working land.

Every engagement starts with a conversation about your land, your goals, and your constraints. From there we scope the work together — no jargon, no padding.

01 — Planning

Farm & land-use planning

Whole-farm plans, enterprise budgeting, rotational grazing layouts, and multi-season roadmaps for new and expanding operations.

02 — Soil & crops

Soil health & crop consulting

Soil sampling programs, fertility planning, cover cropping strategies, and pest and weed management plans tailored to your ground.

03 — Livestock

Livestock & pasture programs

Herd health planning, pasture improvement, winter feed budgeting, and grazing systems for beef, dairy, sheep, and poultry.

04 — Compliance

Regulatory & program guidance

Help with USDA programs, conservation cost-share applications, recordkeeping, and food-safety requirements for direct sales.

05 — On-site

On-farm services

Field assessments, fence and water system layout, planting and harvest support, and seasonal labor for peak periods.

Booking & payment

How engagements work

Plans change — if you need to reschedule or cancel, see our cancellation policy. If something goes wrong with billing, see our refund & dispute policy.
