Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers bookings for consulting appointments, on-farm services, and seasonal labor arranged with Freemen Provisions.

Cancelling or rescheduling

  • 48 hours or more before your appointment: cancel or reschedule for a full refund or free rebooking — whichever you prefer.
  • Less than 48 hours before your appointment: cancellations are refunded at 50% of the booking fee, to cover reserved time and travel preparation. Rescheduling at no charge is still available in emergencies — just contact us.
  • No-shows: if we arrive for a scheduled on-site visit and cannot access the property or reach you, the booking fee is non-refundable.

If we cancel

If Freemen Provisions must cancel or reschedule your appointment (for weather, illness, or an emergency on our own operation), you will receive a full refund or priority rebooking at no charge — your choice — and we will notify you as early as possible.

Seasonal & custom work

  • Season-long retainers may be cancelled with 14 days' notice; fees for work already performed are charged, and the remainder is refunded.
  • Custom-milled feed blends and made-to-order items that have already entered production cannot be cancelled, but are covered by our refund policy if defective.

How to cancel or reschedule

Email contact@freemenprovisions.com or call (555) 013-0142 with your booking confirmation number. We confirm all cancellations within one business day, and refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.
